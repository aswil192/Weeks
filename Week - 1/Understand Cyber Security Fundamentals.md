## 1. What is cybersecurity?

**Cybersecurity** is the practice of protecting internet-connected systems, including hardware, software, and data, from digital attacks, unauthorized access, and damage.

---

## 2. Why cybersecurity is important

**Cybersecurity is important** because it protects sensitive data and ensures business continuity.

> - **Protecting Sensitive Data**
> - **Preventing Financial Losses**
> - **Maintaining Trust and Reputation**
> - **Securing Critical Infrastructure**

---

## 3. Information Security vs Cyber Security

**Information security** focuses on ensuring the CIA triad of information, regardless of its format, whether physical or digital.

**Cybersecurity** focuses on protecting systems, networks, and data from cyberattacks.
- **Cybersecurity is a subset of information security.**

---
## 4. Cybersecurity domains

- GRC: Governance, compliance, and risk mitigation strategies.
- Asset security: Protection of data, information, and physical assets throughout their lifecycle.
- Security architecture and engineering: Designing secure systems and implementing security controls.

---

## 5. Modern threat landscape

The threat landscape refers to the evolving environment of cyber threats, attack methods, and attack vectors targeting organizations, governments, and individuals.

> - Ransomware and malware
> - Social engineering attacks
> - Data breaches and leaks
> - DDoS
> - Zero-day exploits
