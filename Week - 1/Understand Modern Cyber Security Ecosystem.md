## 1. SOC

A **Security Operations Center (SOC)** is a centralized team or facility responsible for continuously monitoring, detecting, analyzing, and responding to cybersecurity incidents. The SOC acts as the first line of defense, using a combination of technology and human expertise to protect an organization's assets 24/7.

---

## 2. Blue Team

The **Blue Team** is the defensive security group within an organization. Their primary focus is on maintaining and improving the security posture of the organization by implementing defenses, monitoring systems, and responding to active threats and attacks.

---

## 3. Red Team

The **Red Team** is an offensive security group that simulates real-world cyber attacks. They use adversarial techniques to test the effectiveness of an organization's security controls, identify vulnerabilities, and measure the Blue Team's ability to detect and respond to threats.

---

## 4. Purple Team

The **Purple Team** is a collaborative approach that integrates the offensive tactics of the Red Team with the defensive strategies of the Blue Team. The goal is to maximize the effectiveness of both teams by sharing knowledge, ensuring that red team findings are effectively translated into improved blue team defenses.

---

## 5. Threat Intelligence

**Threat Intelligence** is the collection, processing, and analysis of data about current and potential cyber threats. It provides organizations with actionable information regarding threat actors, their motives, and their attack methods, enabling proactive defense and informed decision-making.
