## What is the CIA Triad?

The CIA Triad is a fundamental concept in information security. It represents the three core principles used to protect sensitive information.

---

## 1. Confidentiality

Confidentiality ensures that only authorized people can see or access sensitive information.

> **Example:** Logging into your Brototype account requires a password, ensuring that no unauthorized person can view your personal information or performance.

---

## 2. Integrity

Integrity ensures that information hasn't been changed by anyone without permission.

> **Example:** When you upload files to a website, hash validation and logs ensure the exact file matches the file received by the server, without being altered in the middle.

---

## 3. Availability

Availability ensures that information and systems are always ready and working when you need them.

> **Example:** Websites rely on redundant web servers and failover systems so you can access the website 24/7.
