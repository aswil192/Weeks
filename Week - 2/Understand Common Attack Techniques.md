## 1. Phishing

Phishing is a **fraudulent message** (email, SMS/smishing, voice/vishing, QR code) designed to trick a recipient into revealing credentials, clicking a malicious link, or opening an infected attachment. Variants include spear phishing (targeted at specific individuals), Whaling (targeted executives), and business email compromise (BEC).

---
## 2. Social Engineering

Social engineering is the boarder **manipulation of people** to divulge information or perform actions - phishing in one channel, but it also includes pretexting (fake scenarios), tailgating (following someone through a door), baiting (infected USB drives), and quid pro quo (fake IT support calls)

---
## 3. Credential Attacks

- BruteForce / password spraying - trying many passwords. or one common password across many accounts.
- Credential stuffing - replaying breached username/password pairs.
- Pass-the-hash / pass-the-ticket - reusing captured authentication material without cracking it.
- Kerneroasting / LLMNR poisoning - extracting or relaying credentials in windows environments.
- Weakness exploited: Reused passwords, no MFA, excessive account privieges.

---
## 4. Malware Delivery

The mechanism that gets malware onto a system - often the payload stage of phishing or social engineering. Common methods: malicious attachments, drive-by downloads, exploit kids, malicious ads (malvertising), supply-chain compromise (trojanized software updates), and USB drops.

---
## 5. Reconnaissance Activities

The pre-attack phase where adversaries gather intelligence:
- Passive: OSINT, Social Media scraping, DNS/whois Lookup, job postings (revealing tech stack
- Active: Port scanning, service enumeration, vulnerability scanning, and network mapping.
